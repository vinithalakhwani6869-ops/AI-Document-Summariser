FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats are supported?

You can upload PDF, DOCX, and TXT files.

Are my summaries accurate?

AI-generated summaries capture the main ideas of a document, but they may occasionally miss context or nuanced details. For important academic, legal, medical, or financial documents, always review the original source.

Is my data secure?

Your documents are transmitted securely for processing. We do not sell or share your uploaded content with third parties. If you save summaries, they are associated only with your account.

Are my documents stored?

Only signed-in users who explicitly save summaries have data retained. Otherwise documents are processed only to generate summaries and are not permanently stored.

Can I translate summaries?

Yes. Summaries can be generated in the language you choose from over 25 supported languages.

Can I use my own Gemini API key?

Yes, you can configure your own Gemini API key in the backend environment settings.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The free plan includes 6 daily summaries, which reset every day.

How many documents can I upload at once?

You can upload up to 5 documents simultaneously, with each file supporting sizes up to 25 MB.
